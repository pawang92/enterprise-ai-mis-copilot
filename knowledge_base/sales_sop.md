# Sales Performance SOP

## Sales decline investigation
A decline of more than 20 percent against an appropriate
historical baseline should be investigated.

Check regional trends, product availability, sales targets,
and data quality before proposing a cause.

## Reporting
MIS reports should include actual revenue, target revenue,
target attainment, period-over-period changes, and
significant exceptions.

## Governance
Statistical anomalies indicate potential issues.
They do not independently establish root cause.
