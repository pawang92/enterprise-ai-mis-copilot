# Inventory Management SOP

## Low inventory
Inventory below 7 days of cover should be flagged for
urgent review by the inventory planning team.

Inventory between 7 and 14 days should be reviewed
for replenishment planning.

## Reorder policy
Compare available stock with the product reorder level.
A low-stock alert is a signal for review, not an automatic
purchase order.

## Governance
Inventory exceptions must be reviewed by an authorized
business user before operational action is taken.
